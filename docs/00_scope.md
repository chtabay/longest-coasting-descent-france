# Scope

## Research question

Which admissible route in France maximizes the time spent coasting on a standard hybrid bicycle for a 75 kg rider, with an initial speed of 15 km/h and no pedalling?

## Geographic perimeter

The initial production scope is metropolitan France including Corsica. Overseas territories are excluded from the first national result and may be handled as a separate extension.

## Primary objective

Maximize continuous elapsed coasting time.

## Secondary metrics

- travelled distance;
- total elevation loss;
- minimum, mean and maximum speed;
- proportion of time below 5, 10 and 20 km/h;
- number and severity of bends/intersections;
- robustness of rank across parameter scenarios.

## Non-goals for the first reference solution

- recommending that a cyclist actually rides without braking;
- modeling traffic microscopically;
- reproducing every bicycle component;
- claiming centimetric elevation accuracy;
- treating private, forbidden or non-cyclable roads as admissible.

## Two result families

The study should report two separate rankings:

1. **Physical-theoretical ranking**: route geometry plus resistance model, ignoring the need to brake for ordinary bends and traffic.
2. **Operational ranking**: excludes or penalizes routes where intersections, sharp curvature, barriers, poor surfaces or access restrictions make continuous coasting implausible.

Keeping both avoids hiding the distinction between an optimization result and a practical route.
